## Open Academic Reports

Go to **Reports > Academic Reports**. Choose a tab in Report Types. Only reports permitted for your account and campus appear.

## Choose the right tab

| Tab | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Student List | Class rosters |
| Stu. Contact List | Family and guardian contacts |
| Score List | Quarterly score sheets |
| Attendance List | Monthly class attendance sheets |
| Stu. Statistics (Summary) | Campus and grade totals |
| Stu. Statistics (Detail) | Monthly class and session statistics |
| Withdrawn Students | Withdrawal records and approval status |
| Stu. ID Book (MoEYS) | Student ID book pages, covers, and list codes |
| Stu. Transcript Book | Primary and secondary transcript pages |
| K3 Certificate (WIS) | Kindergarten WIS certificates |
| G9 Certificate (WIS) | Grade 9 WIS certificates |
| G12 Certificate (WIS) | Grade 12 WIS certificates and validity |
| Customize Stu. List | Selected information columns and sorting |
| Stu. Profile Label | Student file labels |
| Print Stu. Photo | Photo sheets in two sizes |

## Prepare and export

1. Select the report first, then its academic year and campus.
2. Complete the report-specific filters, such as class, month, education level, or student.
3. Apply the filters and check the preview before exporting.
4. Choose the available Print, Excel, or PDF action. Book reports separate covers from content; certificates use their own content actions.
5. Check the output scope, page orientation, paper size, names, and dates before using official stationery.

A preview may show only part of a large result. Check the row count and scope rather than assuming the visible preview is the complete export.

## Understand saved actions

Viewing and exporting reports does not place students in a different class. Generating ID book list codes, assigning certificate numbers, saving certificate dates, and editing templates are saved changes and require the corresponding permissions.

Use **Help & guides** on a report to open its specific instructions. If a report is missing, ask your administrator to check both Reports access and that tab's permission.
