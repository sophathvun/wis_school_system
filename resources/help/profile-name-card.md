## Open My Name Card

Go to **My Profile → My Name Card**. This is your public professional contact card, separate from student ID cards and examination certificates.

## Preview and share

Review the preview, contact details, and QR code. Correct your personal details in **Edit Profile** before sharing an outdated card.

Use the available controls to copy or open the public link, save the contact, print the card, or share the link through a supported service. Review the destination and finish sharing yourself. A browser may ask permission to open the print window.

## Choose appearance and visibility

Select **Landscape** or **Portrait** and choose a background color. These card controls save when changed. They are separate from the application's overall theme.

Use **Allow public QR card** to enable or disable access. Check the **Public Active** or **Public Disabled** badge after changing it. When enabled, people with the link or QR code can view the public contact card. The card does not show your username or password.

## Regenerate the QR code

Select **Regenerate QR** only when you want a new public link. Review the confirmation: the current QR code and link will stop working. Regeneration enables the card and resets its scan count, so review public visibility afterward and replace any previously shared or printed QR codes.
