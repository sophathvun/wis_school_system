## Open Preferences

Go to **My Profile → Preferences** to find **Theme Settings**.

## Personalize the display

Use the available choices to adjust:

- **Color Mode** for light or dark appearance.
- **Color Scheme** for the application's primary color.
- **Sidebar Color**, independently of light or dark mode.
- **Font Family** and the available theme-base and corner-radius options.

Changes apply as you select them and are saved in that browser's local storage. They may differ on another device, in another browser, or after browser storage is cleared. The Save control is not a replacement for **Save changes** on the Edit Profile form.

## Restore the defaults

Choose **Reset changes** in Theme Settings to restore the default appearance. Review the page after resetting. This does not reset your password, personal information, or school data.

## Customize your dashboard

If you have permission, **My Dashboard** appears in the profile navigation and opens dashboard customization. If it is missing, ask an administrator to review your dashboard permissions. Appearance preferences do not grant access to additional modules or change your assigned dashboard template.
