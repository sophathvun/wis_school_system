## Before you start

Open **G12 National Exams → Bulk Student Photos**. Uploading new portraits and replacing existing portraits require their corresponding permissions.

## Prepare files

Choose the correct academic year and use the filename instructions shown by the uploader. You can choose individual photos or ZIP files. The screen currently allows up to 100 selected files at a time, with larger collections handled through ZIP files.

Follow the displayed limits for individual images, ZIP size, expanded size, and photos per batch. Server upload limits may also apply.

## Preview matches

1. Select your photos or ZIP files.
2. Check the file-selection list and remove unintended files.
3. Choose **Preview Photo Matches**.
4. Inspect filename, match status, student, and existing-photo information for every intended row.
5. Resolve unmatched or ambiguous entries using the available student selector.

Verify student IDs as well as names. Do not assign a photo merely because the suggested name looks similar.

## Upload selected photos

Select the rows to upload. Use replacement controls only when intentionally replacing an existing portrait and when your permissions allow it. Choose **Upload Selected Photos** and monitor the progress and row outcomes.

After completion, open affected exam records and inspect their portraits. Review failed entries before retrying them.

## Troubleshooting

- **Preview button disabled:** select valid files and check the academic year.
- **Existing photo cannot be replaced:** check replacement permission and the row's replacement selection.
- **ZIP rejected or partial upload:** reduce the batch size and read the upload error; an administrator may need to check server limits.
- **Certificate image:** upload it through Certificate Tracking, not Bulk Student Photos.
