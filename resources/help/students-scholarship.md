## Before you start

Open **Students → WIS Scholarship**. Exam setup, candidate management, seating, scoring, review, awards, publishing, reports, and certificates have their own access requirements.

Select the correct exam and academic-year context before editing. Published or historical results are read-only under the workflow's rules.

## Prepare the examination

1. Review the exam settings, grades, subjects, maximum scores, and examination type.
2. Configure centers, rooms, dates, and relevant staff/committee settings.
3. Register or import candidates and verify their identities and grades.
4. Prepare and review seating assignments before confirming them.
5. Preview the required lists, room plans, or answer sheets before printing.

Check the current exam's configuration rather than assuming every scholarship exam uses the same subjects or awards.

## Enter and review scores

Use Scoring to enter marks or the supported transfer/scan workflow. Save drafts before navigating away. A blank score means Not Entered; zero is a valid entered score.

Attendance is recorded per subject. An absent subject is stored separately from its zero score and displayed as absent. Correcting absence to Present requires score review and re-entry where offered.

Every subject must have a score or recorded absence before review. Review scanned or imported data against the source before marking candidates reviewed.

## Select awards

In Result, review totals, subject ranks, exam ranks, and candidate review status. Calculated score ranks are different from the committee's award selection.

After candidates are reviewed, select and save each grade's scholarship winners. The workflow prevents duplicate award assignments and requires a reason for a lower-score exception where applicable. Confirm the scholarship percentage rather than assuming a default is an approved award.

## Publish and export

Publish only after every candidate is reviewed and every grade's award decisions are saved. Published results and scores lock. Check whether a report is draft or published before distributing it.

Use the supported report, result-publication, and certificate actions for the current exam. Confirm student names, award, scholarship percentage, issue date, and selected scope before printing.

## Troubleshooting

If publication is blocked, review unfinished candidates and unsaved grade decisions. If filters hide a candidate, check the exam, center, grade, and student selection. Resolve the actual source conflict rather than creating another candidate or bypassing review.
