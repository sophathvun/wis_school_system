## Before you start

Open **Students → Withdraw Student** and verify the student's active enrollment. Withdrawal starts as a request; creating the request does not immediately make the student officially withdrawn.

## Create the request

1. Choose the relevant academic year and campus/class scope.
2. Find and verify the student or intended students.
3. Complete the withdrawal reason, effective date, requester/contact information, and other required fields.
4. Review the information and create the request.
5. Check that the request is recorded as pending.

Review the selected-student or class scope carefully before creating several requests at once.

## Print and obtain signed approval

Open the available withdrawal form and verify the student and parent/requester details. Complete the paper-signature process required by the school.

Record the signed Principal approval using the appropriate action. The system requires confirmation that the paper is signed before moving the request to the Principal-approved state.

## Final approval

The final approval action requires the signed Principal approval to be recorded first. Only the completed approval produces the official withdrawn enrollment state used by Re-entry.

Pending, rejected, and cancelled requests do not mean the student has left active enrollment. Do not create a replacement enrollment merely because a withdrawal request exists.

## Reject, cancel, or correct

Use the workflow's available rejection, cancellation, or editing actions for the request's current state. Enter the required reason and review the resulting history. Approved withdrawals have different restrictions from pending requests.

## Verify the outcome

Check request status, effective date, reason, and the student's enrollment. If an approved withdrawn student returns, use **Student Re-entry** rather than deleting their withdrawal history.
