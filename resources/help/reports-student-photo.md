## Select students and photo size

1. Open **Reports > Academic Reports > Print Stu. Photo**.
2. Select the academic year and campus, then narrow by grade/class.
3. Choose a student for individual output or keep All Students.
4. Review the photo preview and choose **4 x 6** or **3 x 4** from the Print options.
5. Use **Export Excel** when a spreadsheet output is needed.

## Verify the photo sheet

Check student IDs, names, photo clarity, and the selected size. Use the print dialog's correct paper and scaling settings, then test a sample before printing the batch.

Missing or incorrect photos must be corrected through student photo upload. This report does not import or replace student photos.
