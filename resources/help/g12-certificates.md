## Before you start

Open **G12 National Exams → Certificate Tracking**. Document viewing, uploading, collection management, and correction are separate permissions.

G12 tracks **provisional** and **original** certificates separately. Each type has its own certificate copy, receipt evidence, number, dates, and collection status. Verify the type before saving.

## Find the student

1. Select the academic year and available campus, class, center, result, certificate-type, or certificate-status filters.
2. Search by student ID or name.
3. Verify the student's identity, exam details, Letter Grade, and result.
4. Check existing documents and history before uploading or correcting anything.

Failed students cannot be issued certificates through this workflow. If the result is pending or inconsistent with the official source, resolve it before treating the certificate as ready for issuance.

## Upload the school's certificate copy

Open the appropriate certificate action and review **MoEYS Copy**, which represents the certificate received by the school. Choose the correct image or PDF and review the number and collection details before saving.

Certificate images are optimized as WebP masters with thumbnails. Image downloads can be generated as high-quality JPG. A PDF remains a document rather than being treated as a student portrait.

## Record school receipt

Review **Status**, **Certificate Number**, and **School Received Date**. Use the status that accurately describes the certificate's current availability, then choose **Save Certificate**.

Do not copy a provisional certificate number or date onto the original type without checking its source.

## Record student collection

1. Open the correct certificate type.
2. Attach the **Student-Signed Receipt** copy or PDF.
3. Choose the collected status.
4. Enter **Student Received Date** and **Received By**.
5. Save and verify the tracking row and history.

Collected status requires the received date, recipient, and signed receipt evidence. The student's collection date cannot precede the school's received date. When the certificate is not collected, recipient and collection-date fields must be cleared.

## Correct a collected record

Corrections require collection-correction permission and a reason of at least five characters. Explain the actual correction and review the saved history. If the record changed elsewhere, reopen it before saving.

Previously issued certificate numbers and original evidence are official history. Review the source rather than replacing numbers to fit another format.
