## Before you start

Open **G9 National Exams → G9 Bulk Stu. Photo**. Uploading new photos and replacing existing photos may require different permissions.

## Prepare and preview

1. Select the correct academic year and available scope.
2. Read the upload instructions on the screen for accepted files and naming requirements.
3. Choose the student photo files.
4. Run the preview.
5. Review each matched student ID and name before confirming.

Do not rely on similar names alone. Inspect unmatched, ambiguous, or replacement entries and resolve them before confirming a bulk operation.

## Confirm and verify

Confirm the reviewed upload, then open the affected candidates and check their photos. If replacing an existing photo is unavailable, check the replacement permission rather than uploading under a different student's identity.

## Certificates are different documents

Student portrait photos belong in the candidate photo workflow. Provisional certificates and received-certificate evidence belong in **G9 Certificate Tracking**. Do not mix these document types in one bulk photo upload.
