## Prepare a monthly attendance sheet

1. Open **Reports > Academic Reports > Attendance List**.
2. Select the academic year, campus, and grade/class.
3. Select the intended month. The month controls the days printed on the sheet.
4. Choose selected class, selected classes, or all classes as the Print Scope.
5. Review the preview and use Print, Excel, or PDF.

## Keep attendance workflows separate

This report prepares the class attendance list. Use **Attendance** to record or correct attendance and to review attendance detail or statistics. Daily attendance uses P for Present, EX for Excused Absence, UX for Unexcused Absence, and T for Tardy in stored daily records; report legends may use shortened display labels.

Check both the month and academic year before printing. An empty roster usually requires correcting the report scope or enrollment data.
