## Before you start

Open **G12 National Exams → Reports**. The workspace includes **Grade A Certificate**, **Passed/Failed Graph**, and **Passed/Failed Statistics**. Exporting or saving certificate layouts requires the appropriate export permission.

## Choose the report context

1. Select the academic year.
2. Apply the available campus and student filters.
3. Expand the Reports navigation if its labels are collapsed.
4. Choose the required report tab.

Review the selected scope before interpreting totals. A filtered campus view is different from an all-campus view.

## Passed/Failed Graph

Open the graph tab to compare the outcomes offered by the current screen. Check each graph's labels, values, percentages, and selected grouping. Use available export controls to save a graph only after reviewing its scope.

Do not infer that a missing result is a failed result. Check pending records and compare statistics that use the same filters and denominator.

## Passed/Failed Statistics

Open the statistics tab and choose the required available statistics view. Review totals and any gender, class, campus, or examination grouping shown by the table before printing or exporting it.

For individual results and the specific Grade A or DoEYS result report options, use **Result** and its report controls.

## Grade A Certificate

This tab is a separate school certificate workflow for eligible Grade A students. Review the Grade A certificate guide before assigning numbers, editing the design, or generating a PDF. It does not replace the MoEYS provisional/original certificate collection records.

## Troubleshooting

- **No data:** select an academic year and check campus/student filters.
- **Export unavailable:** check export permission for the relevant report workspace.
- **Unexpected count:** compare the current filters and the report's grade eligibility.
