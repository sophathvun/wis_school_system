## Review summary statistics

1. Open **Reports > Academic Reports > Stu. Statistics (Summary)**.
2. Select the academic year and campus scope. Apply additional available enrollment filters where needed.
3. Review campus rows, grade columns, student totals, and new-student totals.
4. Use Print, Excel, or PDF for the selected scope.

## Understand the totals

The summary counts distinct students within each campus and grade group. New-student totals use the enrollment's student type. These are enrollment statistics rather than National Exam pass/fail results.

## Investigate an unexpected total

Check academic period, campus, grade, enrollment status, and student type in the source records. Compare reports using identical filters; different scopes can produce different totals.
