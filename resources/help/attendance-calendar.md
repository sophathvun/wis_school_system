## Before you start

Open **Attendance → Attendance Settings**. Tabs have separate viewing permissions; saving school weekdays and holidays requires calendar-management permission for the campus.

## Set School Week/Day

1. Open **School Week/Day**.
2. Select the academic year and campus.
3. Review the School Weekdays selection.
4. Select the weekdays on which the campus operates.
5. Save using the page's save action and verify the result.

The default is Monday–Friday with no holidays configured. Review the actual school calendar rather than relying on that default for every campus or year.

## Maintain Public Holiday

Open **Public Holiday** and use **Add Holiday**. Enter Holiday Date and Holiday Description, then choose **Add Holiday** in the form. Review the list and save the calendar changes using the available action.

School weekdays and public holidays affect approved leave and the calendar-dependent attendance calculations. Check the year and campus before changing them.

## Understand schedule-based attendance

The **Schedule-based Subject Attendance** tab explains the relationship to **Student and Teacher Schedules**. Published sessions appear in Subject Attendance on their scheduled dates.

Use dated revisions when changing a published session. Existing attendance stays linked to the original schedule. Opening the schedule page still requires its own permission.

Admin-Daily Attendance works independently of teacher schedules; publishing a timetable does not replace daily marks.

## Verify a calendar change

Reopen the same year and campus and check the saved weekdays and holidays. If leave or statistics looks unexpected, compare the selected date range against this calendar before changing attendance records.
