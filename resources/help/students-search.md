## Before you start

Open **Students → Search Students**. Search access and access to related profiles or actions depend on your permissions and campus scope.

## Find the student

1. Select the relevant academic year and available campus, grade, class, or group filters.
2. Search using a Student ID or the student's name.
3. If several records match, compare Khmer and English names and the enrollment context.
4. Open the correct result using the available profile or detail action.

Clear restrictive filters if the expected student is missing. Do not assume a student no longer exists simply because they are not in the currently selected year or class.

## Verify identity

Check Student ID, name, photo, campus, and class. Use family contacts or history only where your permissions allow it. Similar names alone are not enough to identify a student.

## Make a correction in the right place

Use Student Enrollment for authorized enrollment/profile maintenance, Family Management for shared family data, and the dedicated academic workflow for promotion, transfer, withdrawal, or re-entry.

Search results are not a reason to create a duplicate record. Confirm whether the student already exists before registering them again.

## Common problems

- **No match:** check spelling, Student ID, year, campus, and class filters.
- **Several years shown:** review enrollment history rather than assuming duplicate student identities.
- **Action unavailable:** check the specific profile, editing, or workflow permission.
