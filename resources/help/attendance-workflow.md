## Choose the right Attendance workspace

Open the **Attendance** main menu. Your permissions determine which workspaces and actions are available.

| Workspace | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Subject Attendance | A teacher's scheduled subject session on a specific date |
| Admin-Daily Attendance | One daily attendance status per student and class |
| Attendance Reports | Student absent/late detail and attendance statistics |
| Attendance Settings | School weekdays, public holidays, and schedule information |

Student Attendance is separate from the HRM Staff Attendance menu and national examination attendance.

## Recommended preparation

1. Check the academic year, campus, class, and student enrollment.
2. Review school weekdays and public holidays in Attendance Settings.
3. Ensure subject teaching schedules have been published if teachers will take subject attendance.
4. Select the intended date before recording marks.

## Subject and daily attendance are different

Subject attendance records a particular scheduled session. Daily attendance is maintained independently and counts attendance days. Missing several subject periods does not automatically create several daily absence days.

Subject marks are not saved automatically: use **Save Draft** or **Submit Attendance**. Admin-Daily Attendance saves changes automatically and displays the save outcome. Check that outcome before leaving the page.

## Approved leave

Record approved student leave from Admin-Daily Attendance. Leave follows the selected year/campus school calendar. Existing attendance is not replaced, and future approved leave appears as excused absence when due.

## Access and reporting

Permission to view a workspace does not automatically allow editing, deleting, reopening, recording leave, or exporting. Report access may cover all campuses under the configured reporting rules, while writing remains limited to permitted campuses.
