## Before you start

You need access to **G9 National Exams → List of Candidate**. Creating or editing candidates requires additional permissions.

## Find a candidate

1. Open **List of Candidate**.
2. Select the academic year.
3. Narrow the list using the available center, campus, room, or student filters.
4. Search with a student ID or name.
5. Check the student ID and Khmer name before editing.

## Prepare examination details

Review the student's campus, gender, date of birth, exam language, exam center, room number, seat number, school, and exam date. If editing is available, correct the appropriate field and save. Follow any validation messages.

Exam language affects score calculations and subject eligibility. Room and seat values affect the ordering of printed candidate and result lists.

## Print a candidate document

Use the available Print, Excel, or PDF options and choose the required candidate document. Check the selected year and filters before opening the report. The available document types depend on your permissions.

## Common problems

- **Student not found:** clear restrictive filters and check the academic year and campus access.
- **Edit action unavailable:** ask your administrator to check candidate editing permissions.
- **Wrong room or seat in a report:** verify the candidate's exam details and reopen the report.
