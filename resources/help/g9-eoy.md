## Before you start

You need access to **Assign EOY Average**. Recording an average requires the relevant assignment permission.

## Assign an average

1. Open **G9 National Exams → Assign EOY Average**.
2. Select the academic year and available filters.
3. Find the student and check the student ID and campus.
4. Enter the end-of-year average in the available field.
5. Save and check the displayed value.

The EOY average contributes to the examination score summary. Verify it before reviewing the final result or issuing a certificate.

## Print annual score documents

Use the screen's available cover or score-card report actions. Review the candidate selection, averages, and signing names before printing.

Campus compiler and director names are managed in **G9 Setting → Campus Names**. Year-specific names take priority over all-year defaults.

## Common problems

- **Read-only average:** check your assignment permission and permitted campuses.
- **Wrong signing name:** check the campus and academic year in Campus Names.
- **Incomplete result:** review both the EOY average and required subject scores in Scoring.
