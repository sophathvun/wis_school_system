## Open Edit Profile

Open the account menu, choose **My Profile**, and select **Edit Profile**.

## Update your personal information

1. Review **Staff Name**, **Gender**, **Date of Birth**, **Phone Number**, and **Email**.
2. Correct the editable fields. Email and username must not already belong to another account.
3. Choose **Login Method**: Username only, Email only, or Username or Email.
4. Review your username and email so you know which sign-in identifier will work after saving.
5. Select **Save changes** and check the success message.

Staff ID, position, department, and role are read-only here. Ask an administrator when they need updating.

## Change your password

Enter **New Password** and the same value in **Confirm Password**. The password must contain at least **8 characters**. Leave both fields blank when keeping your current password, unless the system requires you to replace a temporary password.

Save the profile to apply the change. When replacing a required temporary password, the system returns you to the dashboard after a successful update.

## Update your profile photo

Choose a photo using the upload area or its supported drag-and-drop controls. Open the photo editor to crop or adjust it, then apply the edited image. Select **Save changes** on the profile form to store the photo.

Accepted uploads are JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WebP, up to **10 MB** and no more than **6000 × 6000 pixels**. If saving fails, read the validation message and correct the file or highlighted fields before retrying.
