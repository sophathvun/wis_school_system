## Before you start

Open **G12 National Exams → Exam Center Settings**. Managing centers requires **Add and edit exam centers** permission.

## Add a center

1. Choose the available add-center action.
2. Enter **Exam Center Name** accurately.
3. Select **Active** or **Inactive** status.
4. Choose **Save Center**.
5. Check the saved center in the list.

The system assigns the Exam Center ID when the center is saved. Do not enter a student ID, room number, or seat number as the center ID.

## Edit or deactivate a center

Open the center's edit action, review its name and status, and save. Check existing assignments before renaming or deactivating a center that students already use.

Inactive centers may not be offered for new assignments. Deactivation is not a substitute for correcting an individual student's exam assignment.

## Use a center

After saving an active center, open **Assign Exam Center** and select the correct year and candidates. Assign room and seat values there; creating a center does not assign students automatically.

## Troubleshooting

If the expected center is unavailable, check that it is saved and active, then refresh the assignment page. Review validation messages before creating a second center with a similar name.
