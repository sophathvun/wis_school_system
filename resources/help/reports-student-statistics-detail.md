## Review monthly detail

1. Open **Reports > Academic Reports > Stu. Statistics (Detail)**.
2. Select the academic year, campus, and report month, then apply the filters.
3. Review the grade/class and session breakdown, total and female counts, new students, and withdrawal figures.
4. Export with Print, Excel, or PDF after checking the month in the output.

## Understand the month

This report uses the selected month's end date for enrollment timing and approved withdrawal history. Monthly withdrawal counts and cumulative withdrawal counts describe different time spans.

## Resolve discrepancies

Check enrolled-on dates, student gender, new/old student type, and approved withdrawal effective dates. A pending withdrawal request is different from an approved withdrawal. Correct source records through the appropriate student workflow.
