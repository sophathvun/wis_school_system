## Prepare a roster

1. Open **Reports > Academic Reports > Student List**.
2. Select the academic year and campus. Narrow the list by grade/class and the other available filters.
3. Apply the filters and check student IDs, English and Khmer names, and placement.
4. Use the preview page controls when the list spans several pages.

## Print or export

Choose the intended Print Scope: selected class, selected classes, or all classes. For selected classes, check each class before exporting. Choose Internal or MoEYS format where offered, then use Print, Excel, or PDF.

## Resolve missing students

Verify the student's enrollment belongs to the selected academic period and campus. Correct enrollment or identity data in its original workspace, then refresh the report. Changing report filters does not edit enrollment.
