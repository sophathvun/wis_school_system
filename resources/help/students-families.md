## Before you start

Open **Students → Family Management**. Viewing, searching, creating, updating, managing family members, linking children, history, and exports have separate permissions.

## Find an existing family

Search and filter the family list using the available name and contact fields. Compare parent or guardian details and linked children before creating a new family.

A family record can be shared by siblings. Correcting shared contact information may affect the information shown for several children.

## Create or update a family

1. Open the appropriate create or edit action.
2. Review family identity and contact information.
3. Add or update mother, father, or guardian details using the member controls.
4. Save and verify the result.

Editing depends on permissions and the current campus links of the family's children. Central access may have broader scope than a campus account.

## Link students and siblings

Find the correct existing student and review their current family before using Link. Verify the linked children afterward. Use Unlink only when the relationship needs to be corrected; unlinking is not the same as deleting the student.

Do not create a second family simply because a sibling enrolls in a different class or year.

## History, status, and exports

Use the available history view to check changes. Print and CSV export are separate actions. Activating/deactivating shared families and deleting a family without linked students are restricted to authorized central users.

## Troubleshooting

If you can view but cannot edit or link, check the action permission and the child's current campus. If deletion is blocked, check linked students and central eligibility rather than removing unrelated records.
