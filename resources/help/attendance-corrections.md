## Identify the source first

Decide whether the problem is in a **subject session**, an **Admin-Daily Attendance** record, or an **approved leave**. These workflows have different correction controls and permissions.

## Correct subject attendance

Draft sessions can be changed by an authorized assigned user. Submitted sessions are locked.

1. Open the correct subject session and date.
2. Ask a user with reopen permission to choose **Reopen for Correction**.
3. Enter the required reason.
4. Review and correct the marks.
5. Submit again after checking every student.

Clearing an existing subject mark requires the subject-delete permission. It is different from changing one status to another.

## Correct daily attendance

Open Admin-Daily Attendance for the exact year, campus, class, and date. Past records are read-only until an authorized user chooses **Edit**. Review the change and check the automatic save message.

Deleting daily attendance requires delete permission. Its audit history is retained; deletion does not remove the record of who made changes.

## Correct approved leave

Use leave-shortening or cancellation controls for the remaining approved leave, with the matching permissions. Existing attendance is not overwritten by a leave application. Review the source mark before deciding whether it also needs an authorized correction.

## Review history

Use **Attendance History** for a subject session or the available daily history section to inspect recorded actions. Subject history export requires history-export permission.

If a record changed in another window, refresh and reopen it before retrying. Compare the latest version rather than overwriting another user's correction.

## Missing actions

Ask the administrator to check the exact permission: subject reopen, subject-delete, daily edit, daily delete, leave-edit, or leave-cancel. Main-menu access alone does not grant these actions.
