## Before you start

Open **Attendance → Admin-Daily Attendance** and choose the correct academic year, campus, and grade/class. Creating, shortening, cancelling leave, and viewing attachments have separate permissions.

Approved leave follows **Attendance Settings** for school days and public holidays. Existing attendance is never replaced by the leave application.

## Record approved leave

1. Choose **Record Leave**.
2. Select the student and verify their identity.
3. Choose **Full day — all applicable periods** or **Partial day — selected periods**.
4. Enter Start Date, End Date, Approved By, and Reason.
5. For partial leave, select the intended absent periods.
6. Attach the permission letter if available.
7. Choose **Review & Record Leave** and review the information before completing the action.

The permission letter is optional. The form accepts a PDF or supported image up to 5 MB. Attachments are for authorized staff access.

## Understand when leave appears

Leave applies on school days using the year/campus calendar. Future leave appears as **E** when due. If a mark already exists, the leave workflow does not overwrite it.

Check the selected date and calendar before assuming that every calendar day in a date range should create an excused-absence mark.

## Shorten or cancel

Use the available approved-leave management controls to shorten a leave or cancel its remaining part. Review the affected date range before confirming. A cancellation is not permission to erase historical attendance or its audit history.

## Troubleshooting

- **Record Leave missing:** check leave-create permission.
- **No excused mark on a date:** check whether it is a school day, whether the leave is due, and whether attendance already exists.
- **Attachment inaccessible:** check leave-attachment permission.
- **Student unavailable:** review year, campus, class, and enrollment context.
