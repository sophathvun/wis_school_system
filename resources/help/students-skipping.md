## Before you start

Open **Students → Grade Skipping**. Campus request creation, editing, submission, central review, settings, templates, and document archive actions have separate permissions.

Campus staff create requests only for students in their explicitly assigned campuses. Global access alone is not a substitute for that assignment. Super administrators retain broader access.

## Create the draft

1. Select the intended student and current enrollment.
2. Review current and requested destination grades.
3. Complete the required request details.
4. Attach supporting documents and review the request form.
5. Save the draft and check its displayed status.

Check for an existing draft or pending request for the same enrollment before creating another one.

## Submit the signed request

Complete the parent-signature process and use the permitted Submit action. Review the supporting documents and required approval configuration before submission.

Each request keeps its saved approval-flow mode. Changing system settings affects new requests; it does not automatically replace the flow on an existing request.

## Follow approval

In Current Flow, the established Central Office process applies. In Step-by-step Flow, the saved eligible campus and central reviewers decide in order and the final Vice President approves last.

Assigned reviewers use **My Approvals**. Grade assignments use the student's current grade. One eligible reviewer can complete a step; rejection ends the workflow. Follow the request's actual status rather than assuming that printing an approval form completes approval.

## Verify the academic change

After approval, inspect the request outcome and resulting student grade/enrollment history. Do not manually apply an extra promotion to duplicate an approved grade-skipping change.

## Settings and document archive

Authorized users maintain Approval Flow, Approval Settings, committee names, signature/stamp, and form templates. Reviewers and signers must meet the configured campus and Central requirements.

The Supporting Document Archive has separate backup, deletion, and restore controls. Follow its verification and external-copy requirements before removing older files. Archiving documents is not approval of a request.

## Troubleshooting

If submission is blocked, check campus assignment, the current grade's reviewer coverage, required documents, and signer settings. If a reviewer cannot see a request, check the saved reviewer assignment and current approval step.
