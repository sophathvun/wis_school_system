## Prepare a MoEYS student ID book

1. Open **Reports > Academic Reports > Stu. ID Book (MoEYS)**.
2. Select the academic year, campus, and Book Level: kindergarten, primary, or secondary.
3. Apply the filters. This report works at the selected book level and campus, rather than a single selected class.
4. Review student names, photos, personal information, and list codes.

## Generate list codes when needed

Enter a starting number from 1 to 99999 and use the list-code generation action if permitted. Codes are saved on enrollment records as five digits, such as **00001**. Generation is blocked when list codes already exist for the selected academic year, level, and campus.

Verify the entire scope before generating codes. These list codes are separate from certificate numbers and student IDs.

## Print the book or cover

Use the Print, Excel, or PDF dropdown and choose **ID Book** or **Cover**. Check the book level and campus on both parts. Correct missing photos or identity details in student records before producing the final book.
