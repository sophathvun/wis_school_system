## Before you start

Open **Attendance → Attendance Reports** and choose an available report tab. The permissions for student detail, statistics, Print, Excel, and PDF are separate.

## Prepare the report

1. Choose **Stu. Attendance Detail** or **Attendance Statistics**.
2. Set the academic year, campus, grade, student, and date range as appropriate.
3. Wait for the data to load.
4. Check student identity and report scope.
5. Review any displayed errors before exporting.

## Choose the output

- **Print:** open the printable view and check the browser's paper and page settings.
- **Excel:** download the workbook for authorized analysis or sharing.
- **PDF:** generate the report document and inspect its pages before printing or distributing it.

The student detail report requires a selected student. The statistics report uses its own tab and filter context; do not assume that a selection made in one tab applies identically to the other.

## Check the output

Review the title, selected student or grouping, date range, statuses, reasons, totals, and page boundaries. Correct source attendance records rather than treating an edited export as an update to the system.

## Troubleshooting

- **Missing export action:** ask your administrator to review the matching report/export permission.
- **Button disabled:** complete required filters and wait for loading to finish.
- **Popup blocked:** allow the report popup and try Print again.
- **Unexpected rows:** recheck the active tab and date range before downloading again.
