## Prepare a transcript book

1. Open **Reports > Academic Reports > Stu. Transcript Book**.
2. Select a regular academic year, campus, Transcript Level (primary or secondary), grade/class, and Report Date.
3. Select an individual student if needed, or keep the class scope.
4. Apply the filters and review the transcript preview.

All required selections must be complete before records appear. This report uses regular academic-year enrollment.

## Print covers and content

Use **Print Cover** or **Print Content**, or the corresponding PDF option. Confirm the level, class, student, and report date before printing. Cover and content are separate outputs.

## Templates and corrections

Template management requires its own permission. Review a sample page before saving template changes. Correct student identity or enrollment details in the source workspace, then generate the transcript again.
