## Before you start

Open **Students → Student Graduation**. Confirm the eligible Grade 12 enrollment and academic year before creating a graduation record.

Graduation records school completion. It is not the same as receiving a G12 national examination certificate or publishing a scholarship result.

## Choose the scope

The form offers **Individual Student**, **Entire Class**, **Entire Campus**, and **All Campuses**. Choose the smallest scope that matches the intended task and review every affected student before confirming.

## Record graduation

1. Open the available new-graduation action.
2. Choose the scope and academic year.
3. Select the Grade 12 student or the required campus/class context.
4. Enter **Graduation Date**. Certificate numbers are assigned only in **Reports → Academic Reports → G12 Certificate WIS**; do not enter a prefix in Graduation.
5. Review the proposed recipients and other fields.
6. Confirm the operation using the form's action.

Do not use a broad campus or all-campus scope to fix a single student's record.

## Verify the result

Review the graduation list, certificate information, alumni information, and the recorded graduating user where shown. Open the affected student to verify their academic history.

New assignments show **Pending Exam Result**. The student's enrollment remains unchanged until a pass is confirmed. You may assign WIS certificate numbers before or after graduation assignment, and print certificates in advance. The same number is displayed read-only in Graduation. Graduating students remain available in the WIS report.

After printing, use **Mark Printed** on the WIS certificate list. Once WIS exam results are known, use **Confirm Pass** in Graduation for successful students. This completes graduation and makes the certificate valid. Only then can staff record student collection in the WIS report. An alumni selection takes effect after pass confirmation.

If the student fails, use **Cancel Graduation**, select **Failed**, and enter the reason. The linked WIS certificate becomes **Voided** automatically. Its number and printing/release history remain, normal printing/export and collection are blocked, and QR verification shows that it is not valid. Voided numbers are not reused. Cancelled graduations remain in the list for review. A cancellation before numbering blocks new numbering for that cancelled graduation.

These are WIS school certificates, separate from G12 National Exam certificates. Existing issued certificate numbers are preserved. Prefix correction is available only while all certificates in the year are unprinted and pending; printing, validation, release, or voiding locks their numbers.

## Confirm passes for a group

Select the academic year in the graduation list, then narrow the campus, grade, or search filters as needed. Choose **Confirm Pass (Filtered)** to review the number of pending students across all matching pages. Confirm only when every included student passed the WIS exam.

The progress bar shows completion as students are processed in groups of 25. Completed and cancelled records are excluded from the preview. Existing certificate numbers are preserved. If processing stops, review the message and remaining pending records before retrying; students already confirmed are skipped.

## Correct a problem

Inspect the existing graduation and enrollment history before attempting another operation. Use the available correction controls where permitted; do not create duplicate graduations or change a Student ID to bypass eligibility.

If the student is unavailable, check grade, enrollment status, year, and previous graduation records. Read the validation message before changing the source record.
