## Before you start

Open **G9 National Exams → Scoring**. Editing scores requires the scoring edit permission for the student's campus.

## Enter and verify marks

1. Select the academic year and examination filters.
2. Verify the candidate's student ID, room, and seat.
3. Check the exam language and EOY average.
4. Enter each required subject mark. Use zero only when the actual mark is zero; a blank mark means it has not been entered.
5. Save and review the resulting summary.

## Record an absent student

Use the attendance control to mark the candidate absent and record the reason where available. Absence remains an attendance status even when a particular report includes absent candidates in its failed total.

## Understand final results

- **Pending:** required scoring information is incomplete.
- **Failed:** the completed total is below 260.
- **Passed:** the completed total is at least 260.
- **Absent:** the candidate is marked absent.

For completed, present candidates, letter grades are **មធ្យម** from 260, **ល្អបង្គួរ** from 338, and **ល្អ** from 416. Missing marks do not automatically count as failed marks.

## Review before printing

Use the verification or score-collection report to check entries. Correct the source record rather than editing a downloaded report.
