## Before you start

Open **Administrator → Roles & Permissions → Permissions by Dept.** with permission to manage access settings.

Department permissions are shared grants. Review the users assigned to a department before changing its access.

## Select a department

1. Open **Select Department**.
2. Search by the department name or hierarchy label.
3. Choose the intended item and check the selected label after the page reloads.

The dropdown highlights the selected department. Do not assume that a similarly named parent or child department is the same selection.

## Configure the tree

Use the search field to find a main module, submenu, or action. Each dark-blue main box represents a main-menu group.

1. Turn on the main menu.
2. Turn on the relevant submenu.
3. Enable only the actions and tabs needed by the department.
4. Review the remaining groups before saving.

**Full Access** is a bulk control for the visible permission hierarchy. Review its effect before using it; it is not a substitute for assigning the intended campus scope.

## Save department permissions

Choose **Save Department Permissions** and check the success or validation message. Toggling a switch alone does not complete the save.

## Verify access

Review a department user's effective permissions and campus assignment in the Users tab. A user may also inherit access from a role or receive direct grants, so removing a department grant does not necessarily remove all sources of access.

If an action remains unavailable, check its parent submenu, tab permission, campus restriction, and any module-specific rules.
