## Before you start

Open **G12 National Exams → Assign Exam Center**. Updating assignments requires **Assign exam center, room and seat** permission for the student's campus.

## Find the candidates

1. Select **Academic Year**.
2. Choose the relevant campus, examination language, selected class, and grade filters.
3. Search by student ID or name if necessary.
4. Review the assignment summary: Total, Exam Center, Room No., and Seat No.

Each assignment summary distinguishes assigned and blank values. A student can have a center assigned while still missing a room or seat.

## Assign a center, room, and seat

Use the available assignment controls for the candidate. Check the student identity and examination context, select the correct center, enter room and seat, then save the assignment.

Recheck the row and summary after saving. Center, room, and seat must agree with the official examination list; do not infer a seat from the student's position in the table.

## Common problems

- **No candidates displayed:** choose an academic year and check restrictive filters.
- **Center missing from the selector:** review Exam Center Settings and confirm that the center is active.
- **Summary still shows blanks:** check all three assignment fields and whether the save succeeded.
- **Action unavailable:** ask your administrator to check assignment management permission and campus access.
