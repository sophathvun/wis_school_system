## Find withdrawal records

1. Open **Reports > Academic Reports > Withdrawn Students**.
2. Select the academic year and campus, then narrow by class or other available filters.
3. Choose **Withdrawal Status**. Approved is the default; other options include pending, principal approved, rejected, cancelled, and all.
4. Apply the filters and verify the student, reason, effective date, and request status.
5. Use Print, Excel, or PDF.

## Read status correctly

A request appearing under All does not mean the student is officially withdrawn. Use Approved when you need the completed withdrawal population. Pending and rejected requests must not be treated as approved departures.

Use **Students > Withdraw Student** to manage requests; this report does not approve or cancel them.
