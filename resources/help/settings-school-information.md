## Open School Information

Go to **Settings → School Information**. These records supply school and campus details used throughout the system, including student placements and reports.

## Maintain school and campus details

1. Locate the campus record and open its edit form, or create a record for a new campus.
2. Enter the school names in English and Khmer.
3. Enter the campus names in English and Khmer. Campus labels must be unique; use the established campus abbreviation consistently.
4. Select the address locations in order: country, province, district, commune, and village.
5. Complete the available English and Khmer address, house, street, telephone, description, and Google Map fields.
6. Upload a logo if needed, choose the status, and save.

The logo accepts JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WebP images up to **2 MB**.

## Check the saved information

Review both language versions, the selected location hierarchy, telephone number, and logo. Check a relevant report preview when correcting information used in printed documents.

School Information and the Administrator's Branding settings serve different purposes. Use Branding for the application interface. Changing a campus label does not transfer its students to another campus.
