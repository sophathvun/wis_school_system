## Before you start

Open **G12 National Exams → Reports → Grade A Certificate**. Students are selected from the year’s overall Grade A results within your permitted campuses. Saving layouts, assigning numbers, and producing PDFs require export permission.

This is the Grade A certificate workflow. It is separate from the MoEYS certificate documents in Certificate Tracking.

## Review the recipients

1. Select the academic year and campus context.
2. Open the Grade A certificate workspace or editor.
3. Verify the listed students, names, overall grade, and Score / Rank.
4. Resolve unexpected or missing recipients in the source exam records before generating documents.

## Review certificate numbers

Use the dedicated Grade A number-assignment action only after verifying the year and recipients. Numbers are assigned in descending Score / Rank order by the Grade A workflow. Do not apply the G9 numbering prefix or manually alter historical MoEYS certificate numbers.

## Edit the layout

Use the available editor controls to position text and review the supported fonts, frame, and other design options. Set the certificate issue date and preview both Khmer and English text for the selected student.

Saving a layout applies to the selected academic year. Review its effect across the year's certificates before saving a shared design change.

## Generate the PDF

Select the intended students, review whether the frame should be included, and generate the PDF using the available action. Inspect student names, numbers, date, alignment, and page boundaries before printing.

If a student is missing, check their overall Letter Grade, academic year, campus access, and the report filters. Do not change a student's result simply to add them to the certificate list.
