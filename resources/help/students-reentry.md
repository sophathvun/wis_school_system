## Before you start

Open **Students → Student Re-entry**. Eligible sources include withdrawn regular-school enrollments from any year, including imported records without withdrawal approval history, and former students whose latest completed regular enrollment was followed by at least one missed academic year. Summer attendance does not remove that study gap. Pending, rejected, and cancelled withdrawal requests do not make an active student eligible.

## Find the eligible student

Search the eligible-student list by Student ID or name. Verify the previous academic year, campus and class. The badge identifies **Withdrawn** or **Study gap**. Choose **New Re-entry** or the student's **Re-entry** button.

## Choose the destination

1. Select the eligible returning student.
2. Choose the regular academic year offered by the form.
3. Select campus, grade, class, and session.
4. Enter the effective return date and any notes.
5. Review all fields and complete the re-entry action.

The destination year must be regular and started or pending. Changing the destination year refreshes eligibility. Existing active or pending placements block another return. A withdrawal in the same year may be followed by a new enrollment, preserving the withdrawn record. A student who completed the immediately previous year should use the normal promotion/enrollment process; re-entry for a study gap requires a missed academic year.

## Verify enrollment and history

The same Student ID and profile are retained. The new enrollment is classified **Old**. Previous enrollments are unchanged. The new re-entry history links to the previous enrollment and, where available, its actual approved withdrawal history; imported withdrawals do not receive fabricated approval records.

Example: a student last completed **2024–2025**, did not enroll for **2025–2026**, and returns in **2026–2027**. Select **2026–2027**, find the student under **Study gap**, and choose the destination campus, grade, class, session and return date. Do not create a new student profile or an enrollment for the missed year.

The new enrollment's state follows the academic-year lifecycle: a started year produces active enrollment, while a pending year produces pending enrollment.

## Troubleshooting

- **Student not listed:** check the latest regular-school enrollment, selected return year and whether a return already used the source. Current enrollments and graduates are not study-gap candidates.
- **Active-enrollment conflict:** inspect the student's existing enrollment before retrying.
- **Year unavailable:** check regular-period and lifecycle configuration.
- **Wrong destination:** review the available correction workflow rather than creating another student identity.
