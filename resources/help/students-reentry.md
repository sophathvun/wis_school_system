## Before you start

Open **Students → Student Re-entry**. Only officially withdrawn students are eligible. Pending, rejected, and cancelled withdrawal requests leave students active and are not a basis for re-entry.

## Find the eligible student

Review the eligible-student list and source withdrawal information. Verify Student ID, name, and the approved withdrawal history before choosing **New Re-entry**.

## Choose the destination

1. Select the eligible source withdrawal.
2. Choose the regular academic year offered by the form.
3. Select campus, grade, class, and session.
4. Enter the effective return date and any notes.
5. Review all fields and complete the re-entry action.

The destination year must be in an allowed started or pending state. A student who already has an active enrollment in that year cannot be given a second active enrollment through re-entry.

## Verify enrollment and history

Check the resulting enrollment and the linked re-entry history. The same student identity is retained and linked back to the withdrawal source.

The new enrollment's state follows the academic-year lifecycle: a started year produces active enrollment, while a pending year produces pending enrollment.

## Troubleshooting

- **Student not listed:** check that withdrawal was finally approved and no re-entry already used that source.
- **Active-enrollment conflict:** inspect the student's existing enrollment before retrying.
- **Year unavailable:** check regular-period and lifecycle configuration.
- **Wrong destination:** review the available correction workflow rather than creating another student identity.
