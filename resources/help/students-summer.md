## Before you start

Open **Students → Summer School**. Viewing, filtering, registration, editing, deleting, and continuation have separate Summer School permissions.

## Register a summer student

1. Search the Summer School list and relevant student records first.
2. Choose **Register Summer Student** when registration is appropriate.
3. Complete the student's identity, summer academic context, campus, and class details.
4. Review family information, photo, and documents where available.
5. Save and confirm the student appears under the intended filters.

Keep the correct year or summer period selected. A summer registration is different from a regular academic-year enrollment.

## Finish a Summer School period

In **Settings → Academic Years**, find the summer period and choose **Mark Finished**. Staff with Academic Year management permission can finish the last summer independently, including a two-month period, without creating or starting another summer. Confirm the action to mark active and pending enrollments Completed. Withdrawn and graduated enrollments keep their existing statuses; the regular academic year is unaffected. Finished summer records remain available for history.

## Update information

Find the student by ID or name and verify the campus before editing. Shared family information should be reviewed against existing contacts rather than duplicated for each registration.

Existing-record edits and document changes may be restricted by campus ownership. If a control is missing, check the matching permission.

## Continue at Western

Use the available continuation or conversion action for an eligible summer student. Review the regular academic year and destination enrollment fields before confirming.

After completion, check the regular enrollment and retained student identity. Do not assume summer registration alone creates the regular-year enrollment.

## Troubleshooting

If continuation is unavailable, review eligibility and conversion permission. If a student already has an enrollment, inspect it before attempting another registration or conversion.
