## Before you start

Open **G12 National Exams** and choose the correct academic year. The menus and actions available depend on your role, department, user permissions, and campus access.

G12 exam records are maintained separately from school student profiles. Check the student ID, year, campus, exam language, and selected class throughout the workflow.

## Recommended order

1. **Exam Center Settings:** create or review active centers.
2. **Student Exam Records:** prepare candidate identity and examination details.
3. **Assign Exam Center:** assign center, room, and seat, and review blank assignments.
4. **Scoring:** enter subject grades and the overall result, or preview a scoring CSV or OCR batch.
5. **Result:** verify Letter Grade, Score / Rank, and outcomes before exporting.
6. **Reports:** review graphs, statistics, and Grade A certificates where permitted.
7. **Certificate Tracking:** record school receipt, upload the certificate copy, and record student collection with signed evidence.

## Review before confirming

Excel imports, scoring CSV imports, OCR, and photo uploads have preview or review steps. Inspect the matched student and proposed change before confirming. An uploaded file alone does not mean all its records have been saved.

## Keep G12 rules separate from G9

G12 scoring uses **A–F subject grades**, **Score / Rank**, and an overall **Letter Grade**. Do not enter G9 numeric subject marks into G12 grade fields or apply the G9 certificate numbering format to G12 records.

## If you cannot complete a step

Check the academic year and campus filters first. Viewing a menu does not grant permission to edit, import, upload documents, record collection, or export. Ask your administrator to review the specific action permission.
