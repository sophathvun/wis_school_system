## Open your personal workspace

Open the account menu in the top bar and choose **My Profile**. The profile belongs to the account currently signed in.

## Choose a section

| Section | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| My Profile | Review your personal, contact, and account information |
| Edit Profile | Update editable details, your photo, sign-in information, and passkeys |
| My Name Card | Preview and share your public contact card and QR code |
| Preferences | Choose the application's appearance in your browser |
| My Dashboard | Customize dashboard widgets when you have that permission |

Use the section buttons to switch between panels. If the navigation is collapsed, use its expand control to show the labels.

## Check account information

Review the information shown in **My Profile**. Staff ID, position, department, and assigned roles are maintained by administrators and appear read-only in the edit form. Ask an administrator to correct those fields or review your campus and module access.

The account menu's **Status** page shows whether the account is active. Active status allows access according to assigned permissions; it does not grant access to every menu.

## Get help

Choose **Help & guides** on My Profile to open these instructions. Use **Edit Profile** for your own details and the appropriate administrator or HRM page when an authorized staff record needs a separate correction.
