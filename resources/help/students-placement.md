## Before you start

Open **Students → Student Placement Test**. Applicants, question management, form publication, assignment, marking, results, enrollment, and settings have separate permissions.

## Prepare the test

1. Review **Settings** and the available subjects or topics.
2. Add or review questions in **Question Bank**.
3. Build the required **Test Forms**.
4. Review and publish the form through the permitted publication action.

Do not assume that saving a question or draft form makes it available for applicant testing.

## Register and assign applicants

Use **Applicants** to enter and verify applicant identity and contact information. Use **Test Sessions** to assign the appropriate published test to the intended applicant and examination context.

Review the selected form, applicant, and session before sharing or starting the test. Do not use another applicant's test link or assignment.

## Review marking and scans

Use **Marking & Scan** to inspect submitted attempts, manual-score fields, or scanned answer sheets as offered by the workflow. Verify scanned matches and answers before confirming them.

An uploaded scan is not proof that its score or applicant match is correct. Correct uncertain recognition through the permitted review controls.

## Review results and enroll

Inspect **Results** and the relevant pass or eligibility information. Use **Enrollment Queue** to prepare passed applicants for Student Enrollment where permitted.

Sending an applicant to the queue is not the same as completing a school enrollment. Verify the resulting student and placement in Student Enrollment before considering the process complete.

## Troubleshooting

If a tab or action is missing, check its placement-test permission. If a test cannot be assigned, check form publication and the intended applicant/session. Review invalidated attempts or conflicting records before preparing another enrollment.
