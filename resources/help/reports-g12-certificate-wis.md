## Open G12 WIS certificates

Go to **Reports > Academic Reports > G12 Certificate (WIS)**. Select the academic year, campus, class, and optionally an individual student, then review the eligible records.

These are WIS school certificates. National Exam certificate tracking is maintained in its own examination module.

## Prepare numbers and dates

1. Verify names, Student IDs, academic year, and class before assigning numbers.
2. Review **Given Date** and use **Save Given Date** if authorized.
3. Check the Certificate Prefix. Use **Assign Certificate Numbers** only for records that need a new number.
4. Recheck the assigned number and date in the preview before printing.

Imported historical certificate numbers must remain exactly as issued. Their original issue dates are used for those certificates. An imported issued certificate can remain associated with a withdrawn student; do not change enrollment merely to make an old certificate appear. Prefix changes are blocked for years with protected historical certificates.

## Review and print the content

Use **Print Content** or **PDF Content** after the report's readiness checks are satisfied. Check names, number, date, QR visibility, and alignment on a plain-paper sample before using preprinted stationery.

**Edit Template** and **Save Template** require separate permissions. A saved template applies to this certificate level across academic years, so review changes carefully. Student values fill automatically.

## Graduation and certificate validity

G12 certificate numbers are assigned here as the shared source for graduation. You may assign numbers before or after Confirm Pass; do not create a second number in graduation.

Assigning graduation before results leaves it pending. **Confirm Pass** completes graduation and makes the linked certificate valid. For a large cohort, use the filtered bulk Confirm Pass workflow in Student Graduation and review its preview before confirming.

A certificate printed before result confirmation remains pending. If graduation is cancelled after a failed result, the certificate is voided and its number is retained rather than reused. Check the certificate's status before release.

## Resolve unavailable actions

Missing actions may require certificate-number, date, or template permissions in addition to view access. If a certificate is absent, check the selected year, campus, class, and source enrollment before asking an administrator to review eligibility.
