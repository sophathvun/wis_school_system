## Open Withdrawal Reasons

Go to **Settings → Withdrawal Reasons**. These settings provide consistent reason choices for student withdrawal forms.

## Maintain a reason

1. Find the existing reason before adding another one.
2. Open the new-record or edit form.
3. Enter a unique **Reason Key** using letters, numbers, dashes, or underscores.
4. Enter the English label and the Khmer label when available.
5. Set **Sort Order**, choose the status, and save.

Keep the key stable. Use the labels to correct wording without treating an existing reason as a different reason.

## Retire a reason

The delete action deactivates the reason rather than removing it. Existing withdrawal forms remain unchanged. Verify the active reason choices in the withdrawal workflow after making an update.

Editing the list does not withdraw a student, approve a request, or cancel an existing withdrawal. Those actions belong to **Students → Withdraw Student** and its approval process.
