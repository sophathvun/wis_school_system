## Open Education Levels

Go to **Settings → Education Levels**. Education levels organize the school's education offerings. They are separate from individual grades and academic tracks.

## Maintain a level

1. Search for the existing education level.
2. Open the new-record or edit form.
3. Enter **Level Name** and **Level Short Name**. Both must be unique.
4. Set **Level Order**, add an optional description, and select the status.
5. Save and check the list.

Use names and abbreviations that staff can identify consistently across academic years. Use display order to arrange choices rather than changing their names to force an alphabetical order.

## Connect programs

Create the education level before creating programs that belong to it. In **Settings → Programs**, choose the correct education level for each program.

Review linked programs before changing or retiring an existing level. Editing a level's label does not change students' grade assignments or automatically promote students.
