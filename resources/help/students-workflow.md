## Choose the correct Student submenu

The current Students navigation has 15 submenu entries. Your account may show fewer because each feature has its own permissions.

| Submenu | Main purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Search Students | Find and verify a student |
| Student Enrollment | Register students and maintain enrollment information |
| Student Placement Test | Assess applicants and prepare passed applicants for enrollment |
| Summer School | Register summer students and manage continuation |
| Family Management | Maintain parents, guardians, and linked children |
| Student Promotion | Move eligible enrollments to the next academic year or grade |
| Student Transfer | Transfer between the available campus or class destinations |
| Student Graduation | Record graduation and related certificate information |
| Withdraw Student | Record and review withdrawal requests |
| Student Re-entry | Return officially withdrawn students to enrollment |
| Stu. ID Card (QR) | Generate QR links and print student ID cards |
| Student Documents | Store and retrieve student documents |
| Grade Skipping | Submit and review an exceptional grade change |
| WIS Scholarship | Manage scholarship examinations and awards |
| Import / Export Data | Transfer student, enrollment, and family CSV data |

## Check identity and context first

Confirm Student ID, name, campus, academic year, grade, and class before changing a record. A single student can have enrollment history across multiple academic years; this does not mean they need a new student identity each year.

## Use the right academic workflow

Use Promotion for progression, Transfer for a change of campus or class, Graduation for completion, Withdrawal for leaving school, and Re-entry for returning after official withdrawal. Grade Skipping has its own approval process.

Do not replace these workflows by manually changing unrelated profile fields or creating a duplicate student.

## Understand access

Some student lists can be viewed across campuses while edits remain restricted to assigned campuses. Creation, editing, printing, downloading, approvals, and imports can require different permissions.

If a guide's Open page link leads to a page with restricted actions, ask the administrator to check the exact action and campus permission. Reading help does not grant access.

## Verify a completed change

Read the success or validation message, reopen the student, and check enrollment history where available. Use workflow history for a correction or reversal rather than repeating the original action without review.
