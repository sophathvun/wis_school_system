## Before you start

You need certificate-number management permission. Open **G9 Certificate Tracking** and use its certificate numbering settings. Confirm the academic year before preparing a generation preview.

## Understand the format

New G9 numbers use:

**Grade digit + academic-year ending + four-digit sequence**

For example, **៩២៥០០០១** represents Grade 9, academic year 2024–2025, sequence 0001. For 2025–2026, the year part is **២៦**.

This format applies to new numbers. Historical numbers already issued to students must be preserved exactly, even if their format differs.

## Review the generation settings

1. Select the academic year.
2. Review the numbering format and next sequence shown by the system.
3. Review exam center order and location settings.
4. Generate a preview and check the candidate order and proposed numbers.
5. Confirm only after the preview matches the intended issuance list.

The configured ordering handles Phnom Penh before provinces, examination-language grouping, and Khmer-name ordering. Review the preview rather than manually assuming the next number.

## Eligibility

Only passed candidates can receive new certificate numbers. Failed, absent, and pending candidates are not eligible. Existing issued numbers must not be renumbered.

## Common problems

- **Numbering action missing:** check certificate-number management permission.
- **Student excluded:** verify that the result is passed and the year and filters are correct.
- **Unexpected order:** check exam center locations, ordering settings, exam language, and Khmer names before confirming.
- **Existing number looks different:** review its historical source. Preserve issued history rather than generating a replacement.
