## Find your workspace

Use the main navigation to open a module. Only menus allowed by your account are available. Expand a main menu to see its pages. On smaller screens, open the navigation menu first.

## Choose the correct context

1. Check the academic year before viewing or changing records.
2. Select the campus and other available filters.
3. Use student ID or name search to narrow the list.
4. Check the displayed totals before printing, exporting, or saving.

Filters on a page control the records shown on that page. A total may change when you select a different year, campus, exam center, or result.

## Understand permissions

Viewing a page does not automatically allow editing, uploading, or exporting. Some actions require a separate permission, and your account may be restricted to specific campuses.

If a menu or action is missing, ask your administrator to check your role, department, user permissions, and campus assignment. Do not share another user's login.

## Use the Help Center

Search with a task, page name, or Khmer label. You can also choose a category and open a guide. The **Open page** button takes you to the related screen; you still need to select the correct year and filters there.

Use **Print guide** to print the article or save it as PDF through your browser. Help articles explain the system; reading a guide does not change records.

## If something does not work

- Check whether a required year or filter is missing.
- Read any validation message before trying again.
- If your session expired, sign in again.
- When reporting an issue, include the page, selected year, action, and error message. Avoid sharing private student documents outside the system.
