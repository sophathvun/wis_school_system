## Prepare a quarterly score sheet

1. Open **Reports > Academic Reports > Score List**.
2. Select an academic year, campus, and grade/class, or choose a multiple-class or all-class Print Scope.
3. Select **Print Type** for Quarter 1, 2, 3, or 4.
4. Set **Score Columns** to the required number, from 1 to 12; the default is 5.
5. Apply the filters and review the roster and headings.
6. Use Print, Excel, or PDF and verify the selected class scope.

## Check the purpose of the sheet

Use this tab to prepare score-list output. Generating a sheet does not enter National Exam subject scores. Use the relevant examination scoring workspace to record those results.

If the preview is empty, check the academic year, campus, and class selection first.
