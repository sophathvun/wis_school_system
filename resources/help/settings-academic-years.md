## Before you start

Open **Settings → Academic Years**. Check whether the period already exists and whether you need a regular school year or a Summer period.

## Create a period

1. Open the new academic-year form.
2. Enter the period name, such as **2026-2027** or **Summer 2027**.
3. Enter the **AY Code** when needed for your school's numbering conventions. Keep historical year codes consistent with issued records.
4. Choose **Regular** or **Summer** as the period type.
5. For Summer, enter its actual start and end dates and select an optional parent regular academic year.
6. Choose the lifecycle status, add a description if needed, and save.

Start and end dates are Summer-period fields in this form. They do not replace each student's enrollment dates.

## Understand the lifecycle

The available lifecycle values are **Draft**, **Pending**, **Started**, **Finished**, and **Archived**. Review the intended period carefully before changing its status. Starting a period affects the previous started period of the same type and can synchronize enrollment statuses.

For a completed Summer period, use **Mark Finished** and review its confirmation. This finishes that Summer period and updates the applicable enrollments; withdrawn and graduated enrollments retain their statuses. Regular and Summer periods are managed separately.

## Verify the result

Check the saved period name, type, code, and status. Then open the relevant Enrollment or Summer School page and select that period. If it is missing, review the period type, lifecycle, and your access before creating a duplicate.
