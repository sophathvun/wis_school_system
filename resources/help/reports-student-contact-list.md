## Prepare the contact list

1. Open **Reports > Academic Reports > Stu. Contact List**.
2. Select the academic year, campus, and intended grade/class. Use other available filters to narrow the scope.
3. Apply the filters and review the student, parent, and guardian information.
4. Select the class scope and available print format, then use Print, Excel, or PDF.

## Check contacts before sharing

Contact values come from linked student and family records. A blank phone number should be corrected in Family Management rather than filled into a downloaded report alone. Check that siblings are linked to the correct family.

Export only the scope needed for the task and share contact lists with authorized staff.
