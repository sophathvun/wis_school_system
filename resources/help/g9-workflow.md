## Before you start

Open **G9 National Exams** and confirm the academic year. Your permissions determine which parts of the workflow you can complete.

## Recommended order

1. **G9 Setting:** review exam center details, City / Province, signing names, and document issue dates.
2. **List of Candidate:** check student identity, campus, exam language, center, room, seat, and exam date.
3. **Assign EOY Average:** enter and verify the annual average.
4. **Scoring:** enter subject marks and record attendance.
5. **Exam Result:** review totals, incomplete records, outcomes, grades, and graphs.
6. **Print / Excel / PDF:** choose the required result report and verify the preview.
7. **G9 Certificate Tracking:** generate numbers for eligible passed students, upload certificates, and record student receipt.

## Verify before issuing documents

Check the selected year, exam center, student identity, marks, and Result Issue Date. Review a report preview before printing an official copy.

## Keep absence separate from attendance

Absent students retain their attendance status. Some reports display absent students as failed or include them in a failed total. This does not make them eligible for certificates.

Failed, absent, and pending students cannot receive a G9 certificate through tracking. Previously issued certificate numbers must be preserved.
